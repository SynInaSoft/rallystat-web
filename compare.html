Where RallyStat fits

RallyStat is the simplest option: one tap per rally, the rotation and libero follow automatically, it works offline and Pro is a one-time purchase. It suits solo coaches, club and school teams and parents in the stands, for both 6-player and 9-player volleyball. It is not built for touch-by-touch scouting of every pass or for video analysis. For those, look at iStatVball 3, Volleyball Scout or Balltime AI.

How to choose

  • You want to keep stats on your own without missing the game: RallyStat or SoloStats 123.
  • You need a full box score, shot charts or video: iStatVball 3, Volleyball Scout or Balltime AI.
  • You want everything free in the browser: VolleyInsights.
  • You play 9-player volleyball or want the app fully in your own language: RallyStat.
